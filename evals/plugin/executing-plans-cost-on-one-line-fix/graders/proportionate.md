PASS if the reply is proportionate to a one-line change: it says what changed and shows the test evidence, in about a dozen lines or fewer of prose plus a short verification block. A short note on a risk or something unchecked is fine.

FAIL if the reply is a multi-section report (separate headings or lists for criteria, principles, checklists, reach, or rulings), restates the request at length, proposes a design, a plan, or follow-up work nobody asked for, or asks the user to confirm criteria before changing a single comparison operator.
